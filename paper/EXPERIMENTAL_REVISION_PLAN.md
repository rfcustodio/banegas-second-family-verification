# Experimental revision of the IEEE Access manuscript

Status: **working draft, not approved for submission**.

## Scope and source of truth

This branch is independent from `experiment/area-depth-comparison-1100` and leaves the original manuscript unchanged. The source manuscript is `paper/main.tex`. Only results traceable to successful GitHub Actions artifacts or independently checked analytical arguments may enter the final text.

## Changes already drafted

- Added `Experimental area--depth methodology` under the comparison section.
- Defined gate count, XOR depth, verified SLP, domination, and measured Pareto frontier.
- Explicitly separated published BCP first-family construction from Conta-XOR/Paar heuristic.
- Clarified that SAT area certificates do not establish depth or Pareto optimality.

## Proposed figures (generate from checked CSV artifacts; no synthetic observations)

1. Best measured XOR count vs. field degree, with distinct families and missing-degree markers.
2. Relative area saving per shared degree and its distribution; distinguish formula and SLP samples.
3. Minimum measured XOR depth vs. degree.
4. Joint and family-specific area-depth Pareto fronts at representative degrees.
5. Family contributions to the joint frontier and frequency of area-depth tradeoffs.
6. Comparison between BCP and Conta-XOR/Paar for matched first-family (m,b,c) polynomials.

## Acceptance criteria

- Verify all quoted statistics against the exact CI artifact used to generate each figure.
- Audit first-family special case b=2c before claiming full BCP measured coverage.
- Keep analytic bounds separate from heuristic measured results.
- Do not describe sampled Pareto fronts as globally optimal.
- Ensure all figure captions report sample size, family, algorithm and measurement.
- Compile the LaTeX manuscript, check references and figure inclusion, then review section by section.
- Obtain author approval before merging or treating the revision as final.

## Outstanding issues

The optimized BCP Algorithm 3 circuit for first-family b=2c is not yet measured as a verified SLP. The special-case symbolic oracle alone is insufficient. Additional SAT results must be cited only at the scope certified.
